# Features included in this prototype

Every feature discussed, mapped to where it appears. (S)=Seeker app, (H)=Healer app, (A)=Admin, (B)=B2B.

## Discovery & trust
- Outcome/concern-led discovery: stress, sleep, emotional, energy, relationships, chakra, focus, spiritual, recovery support (S: Discover)
- Search (S: Discover)
- Chakra self-assessment discovery hook (S: Chakra)
- Curated healer profiles: certificate-verified + "vouched by senior" badges, certification level, lineage/instructor, intro video, modality + concern specialties, reviews, repeat-client rate (S: Profile)

## Booking, sessions, payments
- Session types: single, 5-session package, 21-day journey/program, team intensive/group healing (S: Book)
- Escrow ("payment held until 24h after session") + UPI/cards (S: Pay)
- In-app video call — no external Zoom/Meet links (S: Call)
- Confirmation + reminders (S: Confirm)

## Retention & loyalty (seeker)
- My Journey: progress, streak, healing journal, reminders (S: Journey)
- Wallet + top-up bonus (prepaid capture) (S: Wallet)
- Membership/subscription plans (S: Membership)
- Loyalty tiers (Silver/Gold/Platinum), 10th-session-free punch card, referral rewards, free-course unlock (S: Rewards)
- Academy: free intro, courses, 21-day programs, "learn to heal yourself" (S: Learn)
- Bookings history (S: Bookings)
- In-app messaging (S: Messages)

## Healer tools (SaaS)
- Dashboard: earnings, sessions, rating, lead-flow ("platform lead" vs "your client") (H: Dashboard)
- Shareable profile link + QR — bring your own audience (H: Dashboard/Share)
- Import existing clients — migration flow (H: Import)
- Calendar & availability (H: Calendar)
- Client CRM: notes, session history, follow-up reminders (H: Clients)
- Earnings, payouts, and the fee model: 0% on own clients + subscription, 15%→10% perishable tiered on platform clients, introducer override (H: Earnings)
- Introducer/affiliate earnings (H: Earnings)
- SaaS subscription plan (H: Plan)
- Profile: certifications (verified), package creation (H: Profile)
- Apprentice/mentor: add an assistant who heals under a mentor, 3-way split (H: Apprentice)
- Group healing / team case room: lead + co-healers, shared notes, split payout, shift coverage (H: Case)

## Platform / Admin
- Verification queue: docs → foundation-verified → vouched (A: Verify)
- Review moderation (A: Reviews)
- Attribution & leakage analytics; per healer-client-pair commission (A: Analytics)

## B2B
- Corporate & hospital wellbeing programs (stress/mental-health framing, DMRA-safe) (B: Corporate)

## Safety framing baked in
- "Complementary wellbeing, not medical advice" disclaimers; no disease-cure claims.
